## English

**This project is a research tool, not investment advice.**

- Factors, backtests and metrics produced here are for research and
  educational purposes only. They do **not** constitute investment advice,
  an offer, or a recommendation.
- Past backtested performance does not indicate future results. Any factor
  that performs well in sample may fail in live trading.
- The evaluation gates are deliberately strict, but **passing them does not
  mean a factor is suitable for live trading**. Gates rule out results that
  are clearly not credible; they cannot establish that a factor will profit.
- You are solely responsible for any investment decision made using this
  software and for its consequences.
- The project depends on third-party data sources and large language models,
  both of which can be wrong. Data quality issues, model hallucination and
  generated-code defects can all produce incorrect conclusions.
- Validate independently and consult a qualified professional before
  committing real capital.
